On-device help

About this page

Jicofuw keeps evening replies, habit marks, and Demo samples on this device. This Support page answers common questions about using the app, Reflection Credits, and permissions.

Getting started

  • Agree to Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, then tap Enter. No Apple ID, email, or password is required by Jicofuw.
  • On Today, tap Reflect on My Day to open the evening editor.
  • Type your words, or tap the mic to record and convert them into editable text on this device.
  • Continue to preview (preview does not use a credit), then Use Reflection Credit & Keep to save.

Reflection Credits

  • New installs receive 100 Reflection Credits as a welcome balance on this device.
  • Saving one newly confirmed reply costs 1 Reflection Credit after you confirm the spend dialog.
  • Recording, typing, preview, Demo posts, and habit marks are free.
  • Buy packs from Me → Add Reflection Credits, or Buy Reflection Credits when balance is short.
  • Credits arrive only after Apple verifies the purchase on the system payment sheet.
  • Credits stay on this device and cannot be restored after Delete Account or clearing app data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: tap the mic on the evening editor (Parent Helper Call). If denied, you can still type.
  • Camera: tap Take Photo on Compose or Me.
  • Photos: tap Add Photo on Compose or Me.

If a permission was denied earlier, open iOS Settings → Jicofuw to change it, then return to the same button.

Drafts, history & habits

  • If you leave mid-session, Resume Draft or Start New may appear on the next launch.
  • Saved reflections lists finished evenings kept on this phone.
  • Habits marks bundled activities and book ideas that ship with the app.

Demo Circles

Posts, likes, comments, Friends, and Demo notes are labeled Demo. They only change data on this install and are not delivered to other people. You can Report, Hide, or Block Demo items from the menus in Circles.

Log Out & Delete Account

  • Log Out returns you to Enter and keeps local records on this phone.
  • Delete Account permanently removes replies, drafts, Demo history, media copies, preferences, reports, and remaining Reflection Credits from this install.

Reports

Reports you file in the app stay on this device under Me → Reports. They are not automatically sent to a live review team.
